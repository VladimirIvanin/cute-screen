THE TOOLKIT

Show what you mean.

From a quick note to a step-by-step guide, right on your screenshot.

Arrows

Point to a detail with a straight, curved or elbow arrow. Adjust the line and arrowheads.

Lines

Underline important details with a straight line. Snap to the axes and choose a solid, dashed or dotted stroke.

Shapes

Frame a detail with a shape, block arrow or flowchart symbol. Choose an outline, solid fill or gradient.

Pencil

Draw freely with a precise pen or smoothing. Hold Shift to add straight segments.

Highlighter

Highlight text and details on light or dark backgrounds. Choose an automatic effect, colour and intensity.

Text

Write directly on the screenshot. Built-in fonts, formatting, lists and caption backgrounds work offline.

Numbered steps

Mark steps with automatic numbering. Choose a badge shape and add a caption beside it.

Callouts

Connect a detail to a caption with a straight, curved or stepped line. Adjust the endpoints and caption style.

Conceal data

Conceal data with pixelation, blur or a solid fill. Draw a rectangle or a freeform outline.

Spotlight

Highlight one or more areas by dimming the rest of the screenshot. Adjust their shape and the dimming.

Loupe

Magnify a detail and connect it to its source with a pointer. Adjust the zoom, lens shape and border.

Ruler

Measure distances in pixels or as a percentage of the image dimensions. Copy the length and angle from the settings.

Eyedropper

Pick a colour from the visible screenshot, copy its HEX value and use it in annotations.

Crop

Keep the part of the screenshot that matters. Crop freely or choose an aspect ratio.

Images

Insert a PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG. Build a composition and adjust borders, rounded corners and opacity.

Emoji

Add one of 100 built-in emoji. Search for the one you need and resize it — everything works offline.

Selection

Select and move objects. Adjust their properties individually or as a group.

Hand

Move around the canvas while your annotations stay in place.

Your screenshots stay on your device.No account or cloud processing.
