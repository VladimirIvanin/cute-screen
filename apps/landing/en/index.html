THE TOOLKIT

Show what you mean.

From a quick note to a step-by-step guide, right on your screenshot.

Arrows

Point to a button or connect details with straight, curved or elbow arrows.

Shapes

Frame an important detail. Adjust its shape, fill and outline.

Pencil

Circle a detail, sketch freely or hold Shift for a straight segment.

Highlighter

Highlight key words while keeping the text readable.

Text

Add a caption. Choose its font, size, colour and background.

Numbered steps

Turn an explanation into a sequence with automatic numbering.

Callouts

Connect a detail to a caption placed beside it.

Conceal data

Cover a selected area with pixelation, blur or a solid fill.

Spotlight

Keep one area in focus by dimming the rest of the screenshot.

Loupe

Magnify a small area so its details are easy to see.

Ruler

Show a distance in pixels or percentages and its direction.

Eyedropper

Pick a colour from the screenshot for your annotations.

Crop

Keep only the part of the image that matters.

Images

Bring another image in and combine several pieces in one composition.

Selection

Move objects and adjust their properties after placing them.

Hand

Move around the canvas while your annotations stay in place.

Your screenshots stay on your device.No account or cloud processing.
